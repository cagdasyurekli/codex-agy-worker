# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not accepted in
repository discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

Disagree with ideas using evidence; do not attack people. Do not publish another
person's private information, private repository content, credentials, prompts, or
logs. Follow maintainer requests to pause or move an unsafe discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these
rules. Enforcement decisions consider context, severity, repetition, and risk to
others.

For immediate platform safety concerns, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools.
For a repository-specific conduct concern, open a minimal issue requesting a private
contact route without including sensitive details. Good-faith reports and reporters
must not be retaliated against.
